FAQ

The questions people ask first.

What happens if you ask it to just write the essay?

It says no — and then gets to work with you. The tutor pivots into guided questions, outline building, and evidence testing, the way a good teacher would across a desk. You leave with your own finished work and the thinking that produced it.

What happens to student data?

Nothing — because we never collect it. No names, emails, or IP addresses; no accounts; no persistent profiles. Sessions use randomized, non-attributable IDs solely for technical support. Read the full privacy policy.

Do teachers need district approval to share this?

That's each district's call, and every policy is different. What we can say: there are no student accounts to create and no personal data leaves the classroom — which removes the concerns most technology policies are written around. Teachers, share this page and our privacy summary with your IT director; we're glad to answer their questions directly.

Why isn't there a Math or Science tutor?

Because we're not ready to do them well. Multi-step, visually represented problem solving is still an area where today's AI makes mistakes — and a tutor that's confidently wrong is worse than no tutor. We'd rather cover four subjects with content we can stand behind than six we can't.

Is the free tier actually free?

Yes — unlimited use, permanently, for teachers and students. No trial clock and no locked features at the tutor level. Schools pay only when they want school-level tools: coursework upload, analytics, safety alerting, and scheduling controls.
